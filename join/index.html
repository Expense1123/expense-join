App not installed?

Install it once. Your invite is remembered and the app will offer to join the group the first time you open it.
  1. Tap Download the app and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open Expense Tracker and sign in. The group invite appears.
If the invite does not appear, open the app, go to Shared expenses, then Groups, then Join a group, and type the code above.
